## 2026-05-25 - [Brazilian Legal PII Redaction & AI Log Hardening]
**Vulnerability:** Personally Identifiable Information (PII) specific to the Brazilian legal context (CNPJ, OAB registration, and Processo CNJ lawsuit numbers) was not being redacted in Edge Function logs. Additionally, AI prompts and results were being persisted to `agent_ai_logs` via simple truncation instead of redaction, risking sensitive legal data exposure.
**Learning:** Generic PII libraries often miss domain-specific identifiers. In a legal SaaS, OAB numbers and Lawsuit patterns are as sensitive as CPFs. Common logging practices that rely on `.substring()` or `.slice()` for "previews" fail to protect PII that falls within the kept range unless `redactPII` is applied first.
**Prevention:** Apply `redactPII` to the *full* string before any character-based truncation. Maintain a shared security module that includes domain-specific patterns (like Brazilian legal formats) and ensure all unified calling paths (like `ai-caller.ts`) enforce these redactions by default.
